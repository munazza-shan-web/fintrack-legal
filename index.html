GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
made clear.

Is it really completely free?

Yes. Savings Goals has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertising. All savings-tracking features are available to everyone.

Where is my information stored?

Your goals, transactions, and preferences are stored on your device. There is no account or cloud-sync service. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings. See our Privacy Policy.

Does the app hold or move my money?

Savings Goals records the amounts you enter. It does not connect to your bank, hold money, or make payments. Deposits and withdrawals in the app are tracking entries.

Can I choose my language and currency?

Yes. Choose English or Urdu, light or dark appearance, and PKR, USD, GBP, or EUR. Changing the currency changes the display label; it does not convert amounts.
