A FEW THINGS, MADE CLEAR

Good to know.

Simple answers, before your first small step.

Is Savings Goals really free?

Savings Goals is currently free to use, with no subscriptions, paid upgrades, or ads in this version. Pricing may change in a future release.

Does it hold or transfer my money?

No. Savings Goals is a tracker for the amounts you enter. It doesn’t connect to a bank, hold funds, or make payments. Savings and withdrawal entries are records, not transfers.

Where are my savings records stored?

On your device. No account or developer-operated cloud sync is required. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

What is included when I share progress?

The app creates a progress card and opens your device’s share sheet. Amounts, transaction dates, and notes aren’t included. Your goal name stays hidden unless you choose to include it.

Can I edit a goal or record a withdrawal?

Yes. Open a goal to add savings or record a withdrawal. Use Edit to change its details. Delete Goal is below Save Goal in the edit screen and asks for confirmation.
