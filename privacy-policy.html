Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Savings Goals (“the App”) for Android and iOS, and this website, handle information. The App is provided by AILANGA SCHOOL PROJECT (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, email jiyoo7480@gmail.com.

1. Information the App may handle

Savings Goals provides savings-goal tracking features. Information you enter may include goal names, target amounts, savings and withdrawal records, notes, dates, and preferences such as currency, language, theme, balance visibility, and reminder time. The App uses these local records to calculate progress, streaks, milestones, and suggested saving pace.

The App also offers optional daily reminder notifications. If enabled, the App may schedule notifications on your device.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide the App's features, display your savings progress, remember your preferences, and provide reminder notifications you choose to enable. We do not use information for a purpose unrelated to the App unless we explain that purpose and have an appropriate legal basis or permission.

3. Storage and sharing

The current app stores savings goals and transaction records in a local database on your device. Currency, language, appearance, balance visibility, and reminder preferences are stored locally. The app has no account system or developer-operated cloud-sync service and does not send your savings records to us.

The app does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell your savings information. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, according to your settings and its provider's policies. We cannot access or delete those backups.

If you open a website from the app, the website and its hosting provider may process connection information such as your IP address and browser details. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the message.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Savings Goals may request notification permission and schedule local reminders on your device. It reads your device's time-zone identifier to schedule reminders in local time; this does not require GPS location. On Android, a system restart receiver restores scheduled reminders after a restart. You can disable reminders in the App or manage notification permissions in device settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you delete it or remove the App's data. To delete a goal and its associated savings and withdrawal records, open the goal, choose Edit, select Delete Goal below the save button, and confirm. This removes those records from the local database.

To remove all local App data, clear the App's storage in Android settings, or delete the App and its data on iOS (offloading alone retains data). Operating-system backups may retain copies or restore data later; manage those through your backup settings. Delete any progress images you saved or shared separately. We have no server copy of your savings records and cannot remotely retrieve or delete them. There is no account to close.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the content you send. We use this information to respond and retain it only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Please do not send sensitive savings details. Contact the email below to request deletion of support correspondence.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not require an account or transmit local savings records to us, including when a younger person uses it. Children should use the app with a parent or guardian where required by local law. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the App's design to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your request where permitted by law.

9. Sharing your progress

Sharing is optional. If you choose to share a progress card, the app generates an image on your device and opens the operating system share sheet. Savings amounts, transaction dates and notes are excluded. Goal names are excluded unless you enable them in the preview. The image may be temporarily stored in the app cache. Your selected sharing service handles what you send under its own privacy policy.

10. Website and app-store privacy

This landing page uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, or browser cookies. Fonts and images are served with the site. The hosting provider may process ordinary server logs for delivery and security. Clicking an email link opens your email application; the site does not automatically subscribe you to anything.

Apple App Store and Google Play independently process information involved in downloading, updating, and using their platforms under their own privacy policies. The current App has no subscription billing or in-app purchase integration. Any future feature that changes how we handle personal information will be described in an updated policy before that processing starts, with permission requested where required.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
